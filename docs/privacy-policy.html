Privacy Policy

App: SHADOWit (Android) · Developer: {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} · Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

SHADOWit is an offline English shadowing and sentence-practice app. This policy explains what information the app handles. We keep it simple: we do not run any servers, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

1. Information we collect

None. The developer does not receive any personal information from the app. There is no sign-up or login, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking of any kind.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your text boxes, folders, notes, starred sentences, play history and app settings.
  • Video files and subtitle (.srt) files that you choose to load, so you can resume practice later.

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time from the app's data controls or by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling removes all of it.

3. Files and permissions

  • Files you pick: when you choose a video or subtitle file, Android's file picker gives the app access only to the file you selected. The app does not scan or browse your storage.
  • Internet: used only for the optional features described in section 4.
  • The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, call logs or accounts.

4. Optional features that use the internet

These happen only when you tap the related button:

  • Translation: the sentence you ask to translate (and the target language) is sent to Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com) to get a translation. Google processes this request under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Translations may be cached on your device.
  • Send to ChatGPT / WhatsApp: the selected sentence is copied to your clipboard and a link opens in the ChatGPT or WhatsApp app/website. What happens next is governed by that service's own terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent unless you tap the button.
  • Contact on WhatsApp: opens a chat with the developer. Your phone number and messages are then handled by WhatsApp.

Without internet, all practice features keep working; only the optional items above are unavailable.

5. Text-to-speech

The app uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (for example Google Speech Services or your phone maker's engine). Speech is generated by that engine under its own privacy policy. The app does not record your voice.

6. Children

SHADOWit is a general learning tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us at the email below.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal data with third parties, because we do not collect any. Data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your device protected.

8. Your rights and data deletion

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete on our side. You are in full control: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to delete everything. If you have a privacy question, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and publish the new version in the app and on the Google Play listing.
